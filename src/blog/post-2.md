<p class="xl:w-250 sm:leading-relaxed opacity-70 text-xs text-center">
It all started with boredom.
One random day, I thought, “Let’s apply for SSC Tech entry Indian Army.”
No big dream. No long planning. Just vibes.
I knew one thing very clearly —
my college CGPA was enough, so the SSB call letter would come sooner or later.
But I had no idea when the interview would be scheduled.
So I did zero preparation.
No physical.
No interview practice.
Nothing.
</p>
<p class="xl:w-250 sm:leading-relaxed opacity-70 text-xs text-center">
Why?
Because my real focus was somewhere else 🎯
I was busy with JLPT N3 preparation.
I was planning to give the exam in December 2025 & that was my top priority.
Army SSB was somewhere in the background.
Then suddenly… boom 💥
One fine day, I got an email from the Indian Army.
SSB interview date selection.
My mind went blank 🧠❌
In a hurry, I selected what I thought was the last available date – 13 Dec 2025.
Why that date?
Because my JLPT N3 exam was on 7 Dec 2025 & I wanted some gap.
Later, I realized the truth 😭
There was a second page for date selection…
which I did not check at all.
What a classic me.
But honestly?
I didn’t care much.
Mentally, I was not ready for SSB anyway.
And my goal was very clear from the start:
👉 Just experience the 5 days of SSB.
That’s it.
I knew one simple rule:
You can get rejected on Day 1 or Day 5 — rejection is guaranteed somewhere 😄
So between 9 to 11 December, I did only one thing:
Prepared just enough for Day 1 screening (OIR & PPDT).
Only to try getting screened in.
My hidden agenda was very simple 😌
I just wanted to experience:
🍛 Fauj ka khana
🏠 રહેણી કરણી (lifestyle)
🪖 Discipline & daily routine
Selection was not the mission.
Experience was.
And trust me…
that experience alone was worth everything 😄
</p>
